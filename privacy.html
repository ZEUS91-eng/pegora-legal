Privacy Policy for Pegora

Pegora: Code Breaker Puzzle  ·  Last updated 31 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Pegora: Code Breaker Puzzle ("the app"), an Android game published by The Zeus Labs ("we", "us").

The short version. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. The app has no accounts and no servers, and your game progress is never sent to us. The only outside party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the ads in the app. If Android's own backup feature is switched on, your progress is also copied to your personal Google account, where we cannot see it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, files, or location. There is no sign-up and no user account. We operate no server that receives data from the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on your phone only:

  • Game progress, including completed levels and daily challenge history
  • Statistics such as games played, win rate, and streaks
  • Achievements you have unlocked
  • Your settings, including theme, sound, haptics, and accessibility preferences

This data is never sent to us and is not readable by us. You can clear it at any time through Android Settings, under Apps, then Pegora, then Storage, then Clear data.

Backup to your Google account

Android includes a built-in backup feature. If you have it enabled on your device, the data listed above may be copied to your own Google Drive account so that your progress can be restored when you set up a new phone.

This backup belongs to you, not to us. It is stored in your personal Google account, does not count against your Drive storage, and on Android 9 and later it is encrypted with your device PIN, pattern, or password, meaning Google cannot read its contents either. We have no access to it whatsoever and cannot view, retrieve, or delete it.

You can turn this off for all apps through Android Settings, under Google, then Backup. To remove an existing backup, use Google Drive, where app backups are managed under Backups.

Advertising

The app displays banner ads and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To serve those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, and language
  • Your interactions with the ads shown, such as views and taps

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. We never see the underlying data and receive only aggregate earnings reports that identify no individual. To understand how Google handles this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, asking whether you agree to personalised advertising. You can change that decision at any time from the app's Settings screen, under Privacy options.

On any device, you can limit ad tracking through Android Settings, under Privacy, then Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not build advertising profiles ourselves.
  • We request no runtime permissions. The app needs only internet access to load ads.

Children

Pegora is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will look into it, although as described above the app collects nothing to begin with.

Your rights

Privacy laws in many regions, including the GDPR and the CCPA, give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. For data processed by Google in connection with advertising, exercise your rights directly with Google using the links above.

Security

All app data stays inside Android's private per-app storage, which other apps cannot read. Since nothing is transmitted to us, there is no database of ours that could be breached.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, for example if a new feature is added, we will update this page and revise the date shown at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
